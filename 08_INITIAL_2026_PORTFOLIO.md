# Initial 2026 Portfolio — Editable Starting Point

This file is a starter dataset, not immutable truth. The application should let the user edit/archive every item.

## Current quarter intent

**September–December 2026:** build a solid base across career, entrepreneurship, spirituality and personal systems without treating every idea as an equal priority.

## Suggested initial objectives

### O1 — Religious and learning discipline
**Desired outcome:** establish a sustainable program covering Arabic, Quran and Islamic history/sciences.  
**Definition of done:** a documented routine exists, activity is being logged, and weekly/monthly progress can be reviewed.

### O2 — First professional role
**Desired outcome:** secure the first full-time position or end 2026 with serious late-stage recruitment processes.  
**Definition of done:** offer accepted OR clearly documented active late-stage processes and a maintained job-search pipeline.

### O3 — Australia Ready
**Desired outcome:** be able to trigger an Australia expatriation plan in 2027 without restarting research from zero.  
**Definition of done:** one dossier containing immigration pathways, target roles/companies, English CV/LinkedIn, market analysis, target city, budget/runway and 2027 execution calendar.

### O4 — Learn e-commerce by operating real stores
**Desired outcome:** understand the full commercial and technical chain through real execution.  
**Definition of done:** 1–2 stores/experiments launched, complete infrastructure understood/documented, real traffic/orders or measured validation data obtained, retrospective written.

### O5 — LifeQuest MVP
**Desired outcome:** turn LifeQuest into a testable product instead of a concept.  
**Definition of done:** one complete MVP (digital, physical or hybrid) used by real target users and feedback recorded.

### O6 — Lean Six Sigma SaaS validation
**Desired outcome:** validate the problem and produce a functional prototype/MVP.  
**Definition of done:** problem interviews/evidence + working MVP solving one clear workflow + initial feedback.

### O7 — Skills and certifications
**Desired outcome:** increase professional and personal execution capability through selected certifications and practical projects.  
**Definition of done:** chosen certifications completed or scheduled with measurable progress, and each major professional certification is paired with practical evidence where possible.

### O8 — Physical condition
**Desired outcome:** reach and stabilize the selected physical target with a maintainable routine.  
**Known target discussed:** approximately 70–73 kg, subject to user confirmation/editing.  
**Definition of done:** target/trend achieved and routine remains sustainable.

### O9 — Engineering homelab / portfolio
**Desired outcome:** operate a demonstrable infrastructure that improves systems/network/cloud/security understanding.  
**Definition of done:** documented architecture + deployed services + security/monitoring/automation + at least one publishable project/case study.

### O10 — Vehicle rental business study
**Desired outcome:** make an evidence-based go/no-go decision without committing capital prematurely.  
**Definition of done:** unit economics, insurance, acquisition, utilization, maintenance, legal/tax, risks and downside scenario documented.

### O11 — AI job application agent
**Desired outcome:** build a useful technical tool that assists job-search execution and doubles as portfolio evidence.  
**Definition of done:** working MVP can ingest a job description, compare it with a candidate profile, produce structured gaps and assist application/interview preparation.

### O12 — LifeOS
**Desired outcome:** operate one personal control system for objectives, projects, data, reviews and decisions.  
**Definition of done:** this application is deployed and used weekly with real data.

### O13 — Business/legal structure knowledge
**Desired outcome:** understand when a French micro-enterprise, French company or foreign structure such as a US LLC is operationally justified.  
**Definition of done:** decision memo with use cases, costs/obligations, tax/legal questions requiring professional advice, and trigger conditions for changing structure.

## Portfolio rule

The UI should explicitly warn if more than **3 projects** are marked `FOCUS`.

It should not prohibit it; the warning exists to make resource dilution visible.
