# LifeOS — Product Vision

## Mission

LifeOS is a private operating system for managing a person's life with the same discipline used to manage an engineering or business portfolio.

It converts intentions into measurable execution.

## Core problem

The user has multiple simultaneous domains and projects: spirituality, learning, career, entrepreneurship, international mobility, health, finances, infrastructure projects and long-term personal archives.

The failure mode is not lack of ideas. It is:

- too many parallel initiatives;
- unclear priority;
- information scattered across tools;
- goals not tied to metrics or deadlines;
- no single review loop;
- decisions made without preserving assumptions or outcomes.

LifeOS should reduce this fragmentation.

## Desired outcome

LifeOS is successful when it becomes the user's default control panel for:

- planning;
- execution;
- measurement;
- reviews;
- arbitration;
- long-term memory.

## The 9 branches

### 1. Religion
Purpose: structure religious learning and non-obligatory routines without reducing spirituality to a score.

Primary objects:
- learning topics;
- study sessions;
- religious routines;
- notes/resources;
- periodic reflection.

Typical use:
- track Islamic history/sciences study;
- record study time and completed topics;
- maintain optional routine goals.

### 2. Arabic
Purpose: make Arabic progress measurable and actionable.

Primary objects:
- learning targets;
- sessions;
- vocabulary counts or sets;
- level/self-assessment;
- weekly target;
- notes/resources.

### 3. Quran
Purpose: manage reading, memorization and revision as separate workflows.

Primary objects:
- surah/ayah target;
- session type: recitation, memorization, revision;
- status;
- revision due date;
- notes.

### 4. Goals, KPIs & Decisions
Purpose: the control plane of LifeOS.

Primary objects:
- vision;
- objectives;
- projects;
- tasks;
- KPIs;
- reviews;
- decisions;
- alerts.

This branch also contains cross-domain objectives such as:
- first CDI;
- Australia Ready;
- LifeQuest MVP;
- e-commerce experiments;
- SaaS LSS;
- certifications;
- homelab;
- vehicle-rental study.

### 5. Personal AI Assistant
Purpose: query, summarize and reason over LifeOS data while preserving user agency.

Capabilities:
- answer questions about stored data;
- summarize weekly progress;
- identify overdue items or inconsistencies;
- compare projects;
- draft review notes;
- explain trends.

The assistant must not silently change data or make destructive decisions.

### 6. Finances
Purpose: know financial position and capacity before making decisions.

Primary objects:
- accounts;
- transactions;
- budget categories;
- financial goals;
- net-worth snapshots;
- planned major expenses.

### 7. Health, Sport & Habits
Purpose: measure trends and maintain sustainable routines.

Primary objects:
- habits;
- workouts;
- weight and other user-selected metrics;
- sleep/activity entries if entered/imported;
- weekly/monthly trends.

LifeOS is not a medical device and must not diagnose.

### 8. Personal Documents
Purpose: create a private, searchable personal document vault.

Primary objects:
- document metadata;
- category;
- issue/expiry dates;
- private file;
- reminders;
- tags/notes.

### 9. Photos & Memories
Purpose: preserve long-term memories as an organized timeline rather than a raw file dump.

Primary objects:
- memory/event;
- date/date range;
- place as free text;
- description;
- private media;
- album/tags.

## Cross-cutting concepts

### Objective vs project vs task

**Objective:** a desired outcome.  
Example: "Be Australia-ready for a 2027 departure."

**Project:** a finite body of work supporting one or more objectives.  
Example: "Build the Australia Ready dossier."

**Task:** a concrete action.  
Example: "Compare two visa pathways."

Never use these interchangeably.

### KPI

A KPI is a measurement, not a task.

Examples:
- applications sent this month;
- portfolio projects published;
- Arabic study minutes/week;
- runway in months;
- weight trend.

### Decision journal

A decision record must preserve:
- decision;
- date;
- context;
- alternatives;
- assumptions;
- expected outcome;
- review date;
- observed outcome;
- lesson.

## Product boundaries

LifeOS does not need to replace:
- a bank;
- a password manager;
- professional accounting software;
- a medical record system;
- a full DAM/photo editor;
- a full LMS;
- a calendar provider.

It should store enough structured information to manage and review the user's life, and link or integrate externally later if useful.
